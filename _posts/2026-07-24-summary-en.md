## [Proof of Reserves: Rebuilding Trust in Crypto Exchanges](https://www.theblock.co/post/409524/proof-of-reserves-what-it-means-for-exchange-trust?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss) ⭐️ 6.0/10

The article explains the concept of proof of reserves (PoR) as a method for crypto exchanges to cryptographically verify they hold sufficient assets to cover customer deposits, gaining urgency after the FTX collapse. Proof of reserves is becoming a new standard for transparency in crypto exchanges, helping rebuild user trust after FTX's misuse of customer funds. It could reduce the risk of another catastrophic exchange failure. PoR typically uses Merkle trees to aggregate user balances into a single hash, which is then compared against the exchange's on-chain wallet balances. However, PoR does not verify liabilities or prove that the exchange hasn't borrowed against the same assets elsewhere.

**Background**: Before FTX's collapse in 2022, many crypto users trusted exchanges without independent verification. FTX secretly transferred billions in customer deposits to its affiliate Alameda Research, leading to a liquidity crisis and bankruptcy. This event highlighted the need for transparent, verifiable proof that exchanges hold customer funds as claimed.
