Transcribe.cpp is a new open-source C/C++ speech-to-text inference library that supports 16+ model families and provides maintainer-supported bindings in 4 languages (Python, Rust, etc.). It was developed through Mozilla.ai's Builders in Residence program. This tool enables fast, local transcription without cloud dependency, addressing privacy concerns and latency issues. Its multi-language bindings make it accessible to developers across ecosystems, potentially accelerating adoption of local STT in applications. The library uses ggml for inference and has been validated with full WER sweeps across thousands of utterances to ensure accuracy close to reference implementations. Binary wheels for Python are planned but not yet available.
