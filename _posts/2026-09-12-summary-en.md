## [Uniswap launches StablePair Hook for dynamic stablecoin fees](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-09-10-uniswap-stablepair-hook-414160) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Uniswap Labs has launched StablePair Hook, a Uniswap v4 hook that applies dynamic fees to stable pairs such as USDC/USDT and WBTC/cbBTC. It is live on Ethereum mainnet with two initial pools, USDC/USDG and USDC/USDT. Stablecoin swaps are among the highest-volume, lowest-margin activities in DeFi, so even small fee improvements can meaningfully change liquidity provider returns and how liquidity is allocated across venues. As one of the first production dynamic-fee hooks from Uniswap Labs, it also signals how v4 customization may reshape competition among DEXs and stablecoin issuers. The hook adjusts fees based on price deviation and trade direction rather than charging a flat rate, aiming to better capture value during volatile stablecoin depegging events. It joins other Uniswap Labs hooks such as DualPool, Permissioned Pools, and LitePSM, with more reportedly on the roadmap.

rss · The Block · Sep 10, 15:00

**Background**: Uniswap v4 introduced a hook system that lets developers attach external contracts to liquidity pools to customize behavior at key lifecycle points, such as before or after swaps, including the ability to apply dynamic fees. Hooks are optional: a v4 pool without one behaves like a standard concentrated-liquidity pool similar to v3. Stablecoin pairs typically trade near a 1:1 ratio, so liquidity providers earn thin margins and are especially sensitive to fee design and depeg risk.
