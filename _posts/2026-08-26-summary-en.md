Strategy sold $2 billion worth of MSTR shares and established a $1.6 billion 'USD Cash' pool, without making any new bitcoin purchases. The company's total bitcoin holdings now stand at approximately 840,447 BTC, worth around $66 billion. This move signals a strategic shift for a major bitcoin holder, potentially to raise liquidity or diversify reserves. It could influence market sentiment and investor expectations regarding corporate bitcoin treasury strategies. Strategy's holdings represent roughly 4% of the total 21 million bitcoin supply cap. The company sold shares without purchasing additional bitcoin, indicating a focus on cash reserves rather than further accumulation.

**Background**: Strategy, formerly known as MicroStrategy, is a business intelligence software company that has become one of the largest corporate holders of bitcoin. Founded in 1989 by Michael J. Saylor and others, it has been actively acquiring bitcoin since 2020, using proceeds from stock sales and debt offerings.
