## [HackerRank Open-Source ATS Reveals LLM Scoring Unreliability](https://danunparsed.com/p/hackerrank-open-source-ats) ⭐️ 7.0/10

HackerRank open-sourced its Applicant Tracking System (ATS), and an analysis showed that LLM-based resume scoring is highly stochastic, with the same resume receiving scores of 90, 74, and 88 on different runs. This highlights the unreliability of AI-driven hiring tools, potentially causing qualified candidates to be unfairly filtered out, and underscores the need for transparency and deterministic evaluation in recruitment. The analysis used HackerRank's open-source ATS with a low temperature setting of 0.1, yet still produced inconsistent scores, demonstrating that LLM outputs are inherently non-deterministic.

hackernews · sambellll · Jun 29, 01:44 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48713832)

**Background**: Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are used by companies to filter resumes. Many modern ATS integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) to score resumes, but LLMs operate via stochastic processes, meaning their outputs can vary even with the same input. This randomness can lead to unfair hiring outcomes.
