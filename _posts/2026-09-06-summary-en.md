rss · Decrypt · Sep 5, 13:01

**Background**: Fermat's Last Theorem, proposed by Pierre de Fermat in 1637, states that no three positive integers a, b, and c can satisfy the equation a^n + b^n = c^n for any integer n greater than 2. Machine-checkable proofs are formal proofs that can be verified by software, ensuring correctness without human oversight, and are a key part of formal verification.

hackernews · torutofu · Sep 5, 13:31 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49576343)

**Background**: In Rust, trait objects (dyn Trait) enable dynamic dispatch, allowing calls to methods on a value of an unknown concrete type. Object safety (or dyn compatibility) rules determine whether a trait can be used as a trait object, ensuring that methods can be called without knowing the concrete type.
