## [Claude AI 发现新型类 CRISPR 酶系统](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) ⭐️ 8.0/10

## [Solana 开始测试 Alpenglow 升级，将最终确认时间缩短至 150 毫秒](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/09/23/solana-starts-testing-upgrade-that-could-cut-finality-from-12-8-seconds-to-150-milliseconds) ⭐️ 8.0/10
