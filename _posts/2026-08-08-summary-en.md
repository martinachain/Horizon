## [Tokenized RWAs triple deposits to $7.4B as DeFi contracts 15%](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-08-06-tokenized-rwas-triple-deposits-to-7-4-billion-as-broader-defi-contracts-15-coinshares-411015?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss) ⭐️ 6.0/10

According to CoinShares, deposits of tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) into lending platforms and DEXs more than tripled from $2.3 billion in Q2 2025 to $7.4 billion in Q2 2026. This growth contrasts sharply with a 15% contraction in the broader DeFi market during the same period. This trend highlights the growing adoption of tokenized RWAs as a bridge between traditional finance and DeFi, even as the broader DeFi ecosystem faces headwinds. It signals a shift in investor interest toward more stable, real-world-backed assets, which could reshape the DeFi landscape and attract institutional participation. The data comes from CoinShares, a digital asset investment firm, and covers deposits into lending platforms and decentralized exchanges (DEXs). The $7.4 billion figure represents a 221% increase year-over-year, while the overall DeFi market contracted by 15% in the same period.

**Background**: Tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) are physical or financial assets, such as real estate, stocks, bonds, and commodities, that are represented as digital tokens on a blockchain. This tokenization enables fractional ownership and increased liquidity, making these assets more accessible to a broader range of investors. In DeFi, tokenized RWAs can be used as collateral for lending or traded on DEXs, bridging traditional finance with decentralized applications.
