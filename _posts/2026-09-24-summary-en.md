The upgrade went live on a community validator test cluster in May 2026, with development firm Anza calling it "the biggest consensus change in Solana's history." If it reaches mainnet, it could strengthen Solana's position against competing high-performance chains and reshape how applications design around settlement guarantees. Alpenglow is a complete replacement of Solana's consensus layer, moving validator voting off-chain and freeing roughly three-quarters of block space for user transactions. It replaces the current TowerBFT mechanism, in which a block becomes final only after enough stake-weighted votes accumulate over 32 slots, about 12.8 seconds.

Alpenglow is the next-generation consensus upgrade designed to close that gap by finalizing blocks in 100-150 milliseconds.
