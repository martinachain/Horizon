## [Samsung to More Than Double HBM4 and HBM4E DRAM Output](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/20/samsung-to-double-hbm4-output-next-year-sources-say) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Samsung is expected to more than double its output of HBM4 and HBM4E DRAM, according to sources cited in a report dated September 20, 2026. This expansion targets the next-generation high-bandwidth memory used in AI accelerators. The move could ease HBM supply bottlenecks that currently constrain AI accelerator production, while also intensifying competition with SK Hynix and Micron in the high-bandwidth memory market. It may further squeeze commodity DRAM capacity, since HBM wafers displace general-purpose memory output. HBM4 was standardized by JEDEC in April 2025, and HBM4E is an enhanced variant; HBM production involves complex 3D stacking and die-thinning steps that are difficult to scale economically. Industry data suggests a 3-to-1 wafer conversion ratio between HBM and DDR5, meaning every HBM ramp directly reduces general-purpose memory supply.

hackernews · giuliomagnifico · Sep 20, 17:38 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49778029)

**Background**: High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is a 3D-stacked DRAM interface developed by Samsung, AMD, and SK Hynix, widely used in AI accelerators and high-performance GPUs. HBM is tightly coupled to the host processor die via a distributed interface with independent channels, delivering far greater bandwidth than conventional DRAM. Demand for HBM has surged with the AI boom, crowding out commodity DRAM capacity and driving up memory prices.

## [Pirate Face Uses Torrents to Keep LLM Weights Alive](https://pirateface.co/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Pirate Face is a new platform that distributes open LLM, image, audio, and dataset weights as BitTorrent magnet links, positioning itself as a censorship-resistant alternative to centralized hubs like Hugging Face. The project drew strong community attention, with 504 upvotes and 140 comments on Hacker News discussing torrent distribution and uncensored model techniques. Centralized model hubs represent a single point of failure where weights can be removed due to legal pressure, policy changes, or takedown requests, so peer-to-peer distribution could make open models permanently available. This matters for researchers, hobbyists, and anyone concerned about AI censorship or the long-term preservation of open-weight models. The site advertises magnet links that "can never be taken down," and commenters noted it lacks scripted torrent creation and that its name may hinder adoption. One commenter suggested that instead of distributing abliterated weights, you could distribute only the refusal vectors (a few thousand floats per layer) and orthogonalize activations at runtime against stock weights, which is computationally cheap and already supported by Antirez's DS4.

hackernews · skepticalgenius · Sep 20, 15:16 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49776699)

**Background**: BitTorrent is a peer-to-peer file-sharing protocol that splits files into pieces and lets many users download and upload them simultaneously, removing reliance on any single server. Large language models are typically distributed as multi-gigabyte weight files hosted on centralized services such as Hugging Face, which can remove content. "Abliteration" refers to modifying model weights to bypass refusal behavior, and "orthogonalizing" means mathematically removing a direction (such as a refusal vector) from activations or weights.

hackernews · tobr · Sep 19, 17:14 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49768336)

**Background**: CRT (cathode ray tube) displays use electron beams to excite phosphors on a glass screen, producing a soft, glowing image with scan lines and no fixed pixel grid. Early pixel art was designed for these displays, where artists used dithering and limited palettes to exploit the CRT's blending and blurring. Modern LCD and OLED screens have fixed pixel grids and sharp edges, so pixel art often looks blockier and less blended than originally intended.
