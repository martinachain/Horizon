## [Bijou64: A New Variable-Length Integer Encoding](https://www.inkandswitch.com/tangents/bijou64/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Ink & Switch has introduced Bijou64, a variable-length integer encoding that covers the full uint64 range without requiring an extra byte, unlike LEB128 which needs a 10th byte for the largest values. Bijou64 offers a different size trade-off compared to LEB128, potentially improving performance in applications like CRDT sync protocols where small integers are common, but may be less efficient for larger values used as identifiers or message lengths. Bijou64 uses a 6-bit prefix to encode the length and the first data bits, allowing it to represent numbers up to 2^64-1 in at most 9 bytes, whereas LEB128 uses 7-bit groups with a continuation bit and requires 10 bytes for the same range.

hackernews · justinweiss · May 29, 15:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48323992)

**Background**: Variable-length integer encodings like LEB128 are used in formats such as DWARF and WebAssembly to store integers compactly. They trade off encoding size for simplicity, but often have limitations like overlong encodings or suboptimal size for certain ranges. Bijou64 aims to address some of these issues.
