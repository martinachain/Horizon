## [Apple Pulls Advanced Data Protection in the UK, Creating Two-Tier Encryption](https://macanorak.com/two-tier-encryption-in-the-uk/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple has withdrawn its Advanced Data Protection (ADP) feature for iCloud users in the United Kingdom, reverting affected data categories such as iCloud Backup, Photos, Notes, and iCloud Drive to Standard Data Protection where Apple holds the encryption keys. This move came in response to a legal order under the UK's Investigatory Powers Act that would have required Apple to weaken the end-to-end encryption on which ADP depends. This creates a two-tier encryption system in which UK users' iCloud data is materially less secure than that of users elsewhere, setting a precedent that governments can effectively force tech companies to weaken security rather than fight in court. It raises concerns that other countries may pursue similar orders, eroding end-to-end encryption globally and affecting millions of users who rely on cloud backups for sensitive data. ADP extends end-to-end encryption from 14 default iCloud categories (including iCloud Keychain and Health) to 23 categories; UK users without ADP lose that protection for the additional categories. Under the Investigatory Powers Act, Apple could appeal to a confidential technical panel or a judge, but would have to comply during the appeal process, and public disclosure of such orders is illegal.

hackernews · ReturnoftheHack · Sep 24, 10:39 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49828731)

**Background**: Advanced Data Protection is an optional iCloud setting that provides Apple's highest level of cloud security by end-to-end encrypting most iCloud data, meaning only the user's devices hold the keys. The UK's Investigatory Powers Act 2016 allows authorities to compel companies to break encryption or disclose keys, and in early 2025 reports emerged that the UK had ordered Apple to create a backdoor into iCloud backups worldwide. Rather than build such a backdoor, Apple chose to remove ADP in the UK, reverting affected data to a mode where Apple can respond to lawful requests.
