This warning is significant because these devices are widely sold on major e-commerce platforms, and many consumers unknowingly expose their home networks to criminal activity. The report underscores the need for greater vendor responsibility and consumer awareness regarding IoT security. The FBI and security industry have repeatedly warned about these risks, yet the devices remain available on Amazon, Best Buy, and Newegg.

Attackers build proxy networks by compromising IoT devices like streaming sticks, which are attractive due to their always-on nature and lack of security updates. The report highlights that even without pre-installed malware, poorly maintained devices with outdated Android versions are vulnerable to being commandeered for such abuse.

## [Stablecoins Streamline Cross-Border Payments](https://www.theblock.co/learn/410227/stablecoins-and-cross-border-payments?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss) ⭐️ 6.0/10

The article explains how stablecoins enable cross-border payments by converting local currency into a stablecoin, transferring it via blockchain, and converting it back. It highlights that blockchain settlements can occur within minutes compared to traditional bank transfers taking 1-5 days. This matters because stablecoins offer a faster, cheaper alternative to traditional cross-border payment methods, potentially transforming global commerce and remittances. Businesses and freelancers can benefit from near-instant settlements and reduced fees, making stablecoins a significant innovation in fintech. The article notes that stablecoins are pegged to a reference currency, most commonly the U.S. dollar, and are transferred to the recipient's blockchain address. It also mentions that blockchain explorers like TRONSCAN and BaseScan can be used to track these transactions.

rss · The Block · Jul 31, 01:53

**Background**: Stablecoins are a type of cryptocurrency designed to maintain a fixed value relative to a reference asset, such as the U.S. dollar. They combine the stability of traditional fiat currencies with the efficiency of blockchain technology, enabling fast and low-cost transfers across borders without the need for intermediaries like correspondent banks.
